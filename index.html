About us

Good to know

Questions, answered

How far ahead should I book?

At least 48 hours before your event. Booking early is the best way to make sure your date is free.

How do I secure my date?

Once we've agreed your quote, pay a 75% deposit by bank transfer. The remaining 25% is due the day before your event.

What do you need from me before the event?

A quick phone call and your event's invitation card (IV), so we can plan the setup.

What if you can't take my booking?

If we can't confirm your booking, we refund your deposit in full within 48 hours.

I'm not sure how many guests are coming.

That's fine. Give us your best estimate when you book, and we'll reconfirm the final number with you before the event.

Where do you cater, and how much is transport?

Events across Port Harcourt, and outside it by arrangement. Transport depends on your event location and is agreed with you in chat.

Do you deliver individual orders?

Not yet. For now we cater events, with the cart and our team on site.

How does the November discount work?

Every event held from 1 to 30 November gets 10% off, whatever the occasion.
